# Education Growth - Product Vision

## Mission
Help parents understand and improve their children’s learning progress.

## Vision
Become the simplest and most effective personal learning tracker for families.

## Differentiation
- focus on growth, not score
- parent + child co-usage
- simple UX

# Education Growth - PRD

## Problem
Parents lack a simple way to track academic improvement over time.

## Solution
A simple web app that tracks scores, goals, and learning progress.

## Users
- Parent (primary)
- Children (secondary)

## Features (MVP)
- Student profile
- Score tracking
- Dashboard
- Goals
- Rewards

## Success Metrics
- Weekly usage
- Score entries per week

## Out of Scope
- Authentication (phase 1)
