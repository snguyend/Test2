# Education Growth - Product Vision
Family Growth Dashboard for Learning & Habits

## Mission
Help parents understand and improve their children’s learning progress.
🌟 Product Concept hiện tại
Đến thời điểm này, chúng ta đã brainstorm và chốt được:

✅ Growth-first dashboard
✅ Family Dashboard
✅ Viewer được xem toàn bộ gia đình
✅ Viewer xem được chi tiết từng bé
✅ Parent Notes có cấu trúc
✅ Goals = Học tập + Thói quen
✅ Progress Score = Academic + Habits

Em thấy concept hiện tại đã rất khác với các ứng dụng theo dõi điểm thông thường

## Vision
Become the simplest and most effective personal learning tracker for families.
Education Growth helps families celebrate progress, build learning habits, and achieve meaningful goals together

## Differentiation
- focus on growth, not score
- parent + child co-usage
- simple UX

# Education Growth - PRD

## Problem
Parents lack a simple way to track academic improvement over time.

## Solution
A simple web app that tracks scores, goals, and learning progress.

## Users
- Parent (primary)
- Children (secondary)

## Features (MVP)
- Student profile
- Score tracking
- Dashboard
- Goals
- Rewards

## Success Metrics
- Weekly usage
- Score entries per week

## Out of Scope
- Authentication (phase 1)
